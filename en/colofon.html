Colophon and privacy

What this site does, and what it does not

The Open Data Atelier is a personal initiative of Josephine Linders. The instruments work with public data and are not an official publication of any municipality.

What the site records about visitors

  • No cookies. The site sets no cookies and builds no visitor profiles.
  • Visitor count with GoatCounter. The site counts how often pages are viewed, with GoatCounter. It records per day: which page, the referring site, browser, operating system, language, screen width and country. The IP address is not stored; GoatCounter uses it only to determine the country and keeps it, together with the browser, in memory for at most eight hours, so that a repeat visit is not counted twice. GoatCounter stores nothing in your browser. The data are on servers in Finland and Germany. See the GoatCounter privacy policy.
  • Hosting on GitHub Pages. The site is hosted by GitHub, Inc. (United States). GitHub records the IP address on every visit for security, even if you have no GitHub account. See GitHub's explanation and GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Your own settings. The choice of light or dark, the last chosen municipality and, on some instruments, a list you enter yourself are stored only in your own browser. They go nowhere.

Data about buildings

  • What the instruments show. A few instruments show data per building or address: Offices and energy label C, the Energy label calendar (non-residential buildings), Construction pipeline, Life cycle of construction, BAG quality and the BAG workbench. These are public data: the Basic Register of Addresses and Buildings (BAG, Kadaster), the energy labels RIVM publishes per building (derived from EP-Online of RVO) and the register of national monuments (RCE). No names, owners or users are shown. Homes with their energy label are only shown added up per neighbourhood, not per address.
  • Why per building. Rules on energy labels and construction apply per building. For that work a municipality or environmental service needs the building, not an average. The pages show what the source records. It is not a finding that an owner is breaking a rule.
  • Is something incorrect? The site takes over the source. An error in the BAG (address, intended use, year of construction, status) is reported to the municipality where the building is. An energy label that has been renewed is in EP-Online and reaches this site with a following refresh; that can take weeks. If the error is in the processing, report it via an issue or via Work together.
  • Objection. Do you own a building that is shown per address on one of the pages and do you not want that? Email via Work together with the address and the page. You will receive an answer within a month. If the objection is justified, the building is removed from the list per building, at the latest at the next monthly refresh. It still counts in the totals per neighbourhood and municipality.
  • Retention. The site keeps no old states per building: at every refresh the new state replaces the previous one.

Figures per district from registers

  • What the instrument shows. Crime and nuisance by district shows how many crimes and reports of nuisance the police record per year, for the municipality and per district. These are counts from the police's open data, without persons, addresses or cases. There are no figures per neighbourhood.
  • Lower limit. A number below 5 is shown as "fewer than 5", for every type, every year and every level, and a second district is hidden if a hidden number could otherwise be calculated back. Small numbers can point to persons; the lower limit is there to prevent that. This concerns criminal offences, and this site processes no personal data about them.
  • Road accidents. Road accidents by district counts the accidents Rijkswaterstaat publishes in BRON with location and outcome, per district and per street. Individual accidents are not on the page, a street only from 5 accidents with injury or death in three years, and fatal accidents are shown separately only for the municipality as a whole: a point with a year and "fatal" can point to a person in a small village.
  • Is a figure incorrect, or do you want to object? The site takes over the police figures, live on every visit. An error in a police figure is reported to the police. If the error is in the processing, or if you think the page does show something about a person, report it via Work together. You will receive an answer within a month.
  • Retention. The site does not store these figures: they are requested on every visit and shown only in your browser.

Services the instruments call

Fonts and scripts are hosted on this site itself. Some instruments fetch maps or current data directly from a government service. That service then sees your IP address, as with any visit to a website.

PDOK (Kadaster)
Background maps, BAG, cadastral map (parcels), district and municipal boundaries, national monuments
CBS
Current figures from StatLine and the dataset for third parties, which also contains the police's open data
RIVM
Energy labels via the Atlas Leefomgeving (Atlas of the Living Environment)
RCE
Data on national monuments (Cultural Heritage Agency of the Netherlands)
RVO
Boundaries and characteristics of business parks from IBIS, the provinces' register, via the map service of the WarmteAtlas (Area in view)
RDW
Paid parking and permit parking from the National Parking Register (Indicators in focus)
KOOP (overheid.nl)
Local regulations and official announcements of municipalities via the search service of overheid.nl (Wmo by district, Youth care by district, Construction pipeline, Low-income schemes by municipality)

Made with the help of Claude by Anthropic

  • How the site is made. The Open Data Atelier is made in collaboration with Claude, the AI model by Anthropic. Claude researches sources, builds the pages, and helps calculate and test.
  • Who decides. Josephine Linders determines the questions, the choices and the boundaries, and is responsible for what is published. Nothing goes online without her approval.
  • No AI in the instruments. The instruments work with fixed, documented calculation rules. They call no AI service, so whatever you enter does not go there. That also applies to your own list.

Reporting errors

Spotted an error in a figure or a text? Report it publicly via a GitHub issue, so everyone can see what was reported and corrected. You need a free GitHub account for this. View earlier reports. For other questions: Work together.

Accessibility

The site aims for WCAG 2.1 level AA. This has not yet been independently tested. If you run into something, report it via an issue or via Work together.
